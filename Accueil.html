Chasing The Trane

Your front-row seat to the evolving world of jazz, where classic standards meet the pioneering artists shaping the sound of tomorrow.

Our Mission :

Our mission is simple: to keep the spirit of jazz alive and swinging. We bridge the gap between the legends and the new vanguard, curating the essential sounds and stories for a modern audience. We're here to inform your taste, spark your curiosity, and fuel your love for America's greatest art form.

But, What is JAZZHUB?

Welcome to your all-access pass to the world of jazz. Our platform is built upon four core pillars designed to immerse you in the music you love.

The Discover Page :

Become the connoisseur. Our "Discover" section is your curated listening room, where you can explore timelines of the genre's evolution, deep-dive into essential albums with rich liner notes & enrich your knowledge on the massive world of Jazz Music.

E-Learning :

Become the musician. Through our state-of-the-art "E-Learning" portal, you can unlock your potential with a vast library of video masterclasses from renowned artists, interactive theory workshops, and progressive lessons for every instrument and skill level, from novice to professional.

Store Page :

Make it yours. Our integrated "E-Shop" is a trusted marketplace where you can instantly purchase high-quality digital tracks and rare vinyl pressings, subscribe to exclusive artist series, and shop for a carefully selected collection of jazz-inspired apparel and merchandise.
